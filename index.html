Mission Briefing
NAGT // Nuclear Arms Global Tracker
⚠ Hybrid — real and simulated. Live data: your local clock, real satellite positions (GPS / GLONASS / BeiDou via CelesTrak TLEs + SGP4), seismic events (USGS), upcoming rocket launches with live T- countdown (The Space Devs), active federal hazard alerts (NWS — same feed FEMA reads), and an on-demand satellite imagery basemap (Esri World Imagery — click any facility popup's "📷 Satellite view" to zoom in). Simulated: DEFCON, mobile-force movements, and the launch-intercept animation — those are classified by design and have no public real-time source; this dashboard does not pretend otherwise.
  • 🗺Nine nuclear states on an offline tactical map. Click any nation or sidebar row to fly there.
  • 🛰Satellite & triad-movement tracking (ground · air · water). Toggle from the map controls.
  • ☢Interactive DEFCON scale + arsenal-development trends. Click a DEFCON level to set posture.
  • ⚠Launch simulation with live impact-ETA & GBI intercept. Hit “Simulate Launch”.
